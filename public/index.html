The media server, reimagined

Everything you own.
Everything you don’t.

Pufferfin is a media server built on Jellyfin, with an Apple TV style interface and the *arr stack built right in.

View on GitHub Get started

Designed for the big screen.

Large artwork, a black background and smooth animations. Navigate with a keyboard or a remote.

A complete catalog

Movies, shows and people from TMDB, audiobooks from Audible. What you don't own yet sits right next to what you do.

One button

If the title is on your disks, it plays. If it isn't, Pufferfin goes and gets it.

Everything Jellyfin does

Library, playback, transcoding, accounts, resume and subtitles, all kept intact.

The *arr stack, built in.

Connect your services once from the admin panel. Pufferfin drives them for you, with a connection test for each.

Play or Download
  • Movies

    Radarr

    Missing films are requested and imported straight into your library.

  • Series

    Sonarr

    Whole shows, seasons or single episodes, tracked as they air.

  • Indexers

    Prowlarr

    One place for your indexers, shared across every service.

  • Subtitles

    Bazarr

    Subtitles in your languages, fetched as soon as a title arrives.

  • Books

    Shelfmark

    Books and audiobooks, found and added to the same library.

  • Downloads

    qBittorrent

    The download client behind it all, with a shared download folder.

Built on Jellyfin.
